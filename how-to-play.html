How to Play CricGuess

Your complete guide to rules, clue progression, scoring strategies, and frequently asked questions.

The Objective

In CricGuess, your mission is to identify the mystery international cricketer in 6 guesses or fewer.

Each round begins with Clue 1 already revealed. If your guess is incorrect, that attempt slot receives a red mark and Clue 2 unlocks automatically. Clues get progressively easier with every guess!

The 6 Progressive Clue Tiers

Clue 1 (Hardest): Debut year, career longevity, and total international matches across Tests, ODIs, or T20Is.
Clue 2 (Hard): Playing role (Top-order batter, fast bowler, spin wizard, all-rounder) and batting/bowling style.
Clue 3 (Medium): National team captaincy details, major ICC tournament appearances, or domestic state teams.
Clue 4 (Moderate): Career milestones — centuries, 5-wicket hauls, Test averages, or World Cup achievements.
Clue 5 (Easy): T20 franchise teams (IPL, BBL, PSL, CPL, County) and memorable series victories.
Clue 6 (Final Clue): Legendary records, iconic nicknames (e.g. "Master Blaster", "King Kohli", "Rawalpindi Express"), and jersey numbers.

Game Modes Explained

1. Daily Challenge

Every day at 00:00 IST (Indian Standard Time), a brand-new cricketer is chosen. All players around the world receive the exact same cricketer on the same date. Your win streak increases when you solve consecutive daily puzzles!

2. Unlimited Practice Mode

Want to sharpen your skills? Unlimited mode lets you play back-to-back rounds without any waiting time. You can also filter by:

  • Country: Focus on Indian, Australian, English, Pakistani, or West Indian cricketers.
  • Role: Batters, Bowlers, All-rounders, or Wicketkeepers.
  • Era: Pre-1980 classics, 1980s–1990s icons, 2000s–2010s heroes, or modern 2020s superstars.

Pro Strategies & Tips

  1. Check the Debut Year closely: A player who debuted in 2008 and has 500+ matches narrows the field down to a very select cohort of modern modern greats.
  2. Use the Search Autocomplete: Don't guess wildly! Use the search box to see valid cricketers and their country tags.
  3. Think about multi-format players: If a clue specifies 400+ international games, that cricketer almost certainly had a prolonged career across multiple formats.
  4. Keep your streak alive: In Daily mode, even if you are unsure on Clue 1, using your first 2 guesses strategically helps reveal the critical Clue 3 and 4 tags!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When does the Daily Puzzle change?

The daily puzzle updates every night precisely at midnight IST (00:00 Indian Standard Time). A live countdown timer is visible on the daily page.

Can the same player appear twice in Unlimited mode?

No! Our smart non-repeating algorithm excludes any cricketer you have already guessed until you have exhausted the entire available pool.

Is CricGuess free to play?

Yes, 100% free! No account registration, app installation, or subscription is required.

Where is my game data stored?

All your streaks, statistics, and current daily guesses are stored securely and privately in your browser's local storage.
