About CricGuess

Created by cricket aficionados for the worldwide cricket community.

Our Mission

Cricket is more than just a game; it is a rich tapestry of history, statistics, iconic moments, and extraordinary human stories. From the timeless Test matches at Lord's and the Melbourne Cricket Ground to electric World Cup finals at the Narendra Modi Stadium, cricket connects millions of people across continents.

CricGuess was founded with a simple goal: to celebrate cricket heritage by transforming career statistics and trivia into an engaging, accessible, and cerebral daily puzzle.

How We Curate Our Player Dataset

Every single cricketer in our database is carefully verified against official international records:

  • Accurate Career Milestones: Debut years, matches played, bowling styles, and century records are verified against official ICC records and statistical archives.
  • Balanced Difficulty: Our 6 progressive clue tiers are handcrafted to test both casual fans and hardcore cricket enthusiasts.
  • Inclusive Eras & Nations: Our database spans from early legends (Sir Don Bradman, Sir Garry Sobers, Kapil Dev, Imran Khan) through 90s icons (Sachin Tendulkar, Shane Warne, Wasim Akram, Brian Lara) to modern titans (Virat Kohli, Steve Smith, Ben Stokes, Jasprit Bumrah).

Zero Paywalls, Zero Friction

We believe trivia games should be effortless to enjoy:

  • No mandatory account sign-up.
  • No app download or app store requirements.
  • Blazing fast load times with vanilla web technologies.
  • Fully responsive on smartphones, tablets, and desktops.
