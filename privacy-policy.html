Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

At CricGuess ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible at https://cricguess.com, we prioritize the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by CricGuess and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at privacy@cricguess.com.

2. Browser Local Storage (No Personal Accounts)

CricGuess does not require user registration or account creation. We do not store your name, password, or physical identity on any external server.

To deliver core gameplay features, CricGuess utilizes your browser's localStorage mechanism to store:

  • Game Progress & Guesses: To allow you to resume today's daily puzzle without losing your place.
  • Player Statistics: Games played, win counts, current streaks, max streaks, and guess distribution charts.
  • User Interface Preferences: Dark mode / Light mode selection.
  • Cookie Consent: Your cookie preference choice (accepted / declined).

This data remains exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or sold to third parties. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or cache.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements when you visit our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on CricGuess.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.

4. Log Files

CricGuess follows a standard procedure of using log files provided by web hosting infrastructure (e.g., Cloudflare, Netlify). The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, and ensuring security against DDoS attacks.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all your data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions. Since we do not maintain accounts, clearing your browser localStorage deletes all site data immediately.
  • The right to object to processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.

6. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), among other rights, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected.
  • Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, not sell the consumer's personal data. CricGuess does not sell personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. CricGuess does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.
